## Deliberately not images

Two things in this deck that would normally be screenshots are rendered as slide content instead,
and need no asset:

- **Slide 2** — the Bluetooth signal meter is the `SignalMeter.vue` component, not a screenshot of
  Ben Zhang's terminal. The tweet is quoted as text with a `.src` link to
  <https://x.com/un1c0rnioz/status/2084686552299634805>.
- **Slide 4** — the edgePython figures (14,808 lines, 136 files, 39% C, one week) are set as type,
  not captured from the blog post. `.src` links to
  <https://liorpachter.wordpress.com/2026/02/19/the-quickening/> and
  <https://github.com/pachterlab/edgePython>.
